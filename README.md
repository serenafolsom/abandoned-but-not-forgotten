# Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars

A short film made for the [NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/).

**Created by:** Serena Nguyen, 10th grade student at Silver Creek High School, San Jose, California, USA

## Overview

*Abandoned but not Forgotten* is a short film that uses storytelling to introduce NASA equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars. It combines AI-generated images, authentic NASA photos and footage, narration, and sound effects to tell the stories of these machines, their missions, and their scientific contributions.

## Project Details

### 1. What does the project do, and how does it work?

The film follows a fictional character, Leo, on a magical journey that introduces real NASA equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars. AI-generated scenes, authentic NASA imagery, character voices, narration, music, and sound effects work together to tell the story of each machine, its mission, and what it discovered.

### 2. What are the benefits?

The film makes space history easier to understand and more interesting, especially for younger audiences. It helps viewers learn about NASA missions while recognizing the important achievements of equipment that is no longer in use.

### 3. What is the intended impact?

I hope to inspire curiosity about space exploration and encourage people to appreciate the scientists, engineers, and machines that made important discoveries possible. I also want to show that even when a mission ends, its contributions can continue to influence future exploration.

### 4. What tools and software were used?

| Tool | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Gemini** | Research assistance and brainstorming |
| **ChatGPT** | Image generation, script and dialogue reviews, and proofreading |
| **Suno AI** | Music, sound effects, and soundtracks |
| **ElevenLabs** | Character voices and narration |
| **Photopea** | Image editing and visual effects |
| **Microsoft Clipchamp & CapCut** | Video editing, transitions, and combining visuals with audio |

I developed the story, researched and selected the NASA equipment, wrote the script, designed the scenes, created and refined the AI prompts, and edited the video. AI assisted with production.

### 5. How is the project creative?

Instead of making a traditional documentary, I created a fictional character, Leo, whose personal struggle connects to the idea of equipment being left behind. His magical journey lets viewers discover real NASA history through an emotional story. I also combined AI-generated scenes with authentic NASA imagery to connect imagination with scientific facts.

### 6. What factors were considered during development?

- **Scientific accuracy:** representing historical NASA equipment and missions correctly.
- **Accessibility:** making the story understandable for younger audiences.
- **Balance:** combining emotional storytelling with educational information.
- **Visual consistency:** keeping characters and settings consistent across scenes.
- **Length:** fitting meaningful information into a short film.
